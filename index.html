Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is MockTest completely free?

Yes! MockTest is 100% free forever. There are no hidden charges, no signup fees, and no premium plans. All features are available to everyone.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required. Just open your browser, sign up, and start practicing. Works on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and all modern browsers.

Can I use it on mobile?

Absolutely! MockTest works perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. The interface is fully responsive.

What subjects are available?

Currently we have General Knowledge, English Grammar, Science, Reasoning, Hindi Grammar, Maths Basics, and Computer Basics. More subjects are being added regularly.

How does the fullscreen mode work?

When you start a test, it opens in fullscreen mode to prevent distraction and discourage cheating. You can't exit fullscreen until you submit the test.

Is my progress saved if I refresh?

Yes! Your answers and timer are automatically saved. If you accidentally refresh or close the tab, you can resume your test from where you left off.

Can I retake a test?

Yes! You can retake any test as many times as you want. Each attempt is recorded separately, so you can track your improvement over time.
